# Filee — Offline File Format Converter

[中文文档](Filee.md) | **English**

## Core Features

- **One gesture does it**: hold a modifier key, drag the file, drop it on a target format — a progress ring fills and the job is done, no window to open.
- **268 formats across 17 categories**: images, vector graphics, PDF, documents, HWP/HWPX, spreadsheets, presentations, text & markup, data, e-books & comics, video, audio, subtitles, archives, CAD drawings, 3D models, and fonts.
- **No Office required**: DOCX / XLSX / PPTX are read and written by a built-in engine. Even if Microsoft Office or Hancom Office is installed, Filee **never uses** them.
- **Batch and parallel**: convert 1 file or 100 at once — a failure on one file does not affect the rest, and you can merge the results into a single PDF or ZIP.
- **100% local**: nothing is uploaded, it works fully offline, no ads, no watermarks, no registration, and no limits on file count or size.
- **Command line and automation**: `filee convert` and `filee watch` support wildcards, whole folders, JSON output, and exit codes — ideal for scripts and scheduled tasks.
- **Multilingual interface**: 20+ languages including Simplified Chinese, English, and Korean.

## Downloads

Either method works; the official website does not require a VPN.

Visit <https://github.com/KnifeLemon/Filee/releases/latest> and download the installer for your system.

Visit <https://filee.sh/en/#download> and pick the build for your system. The site hosts the installers directly, which usually feels faster from mainland China.
